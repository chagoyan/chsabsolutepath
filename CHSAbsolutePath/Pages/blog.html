Day 404: The missing folder

This morning a page asked me for https://chsabsolutepath.netlify.app/planet.png.

I drove to the root and looked around: favicon.ico, index.html, robots.txt, site_map.png. No planet. I came home empty-handed.

The planet lives inside the Images folder, and the page forgot to say so. Ten minutes later it asked for https://chsabsolutepath.netlify.app/Images/planet.png, and I delivered it in under a second.

Lesson: name every folder on the way to the file.
